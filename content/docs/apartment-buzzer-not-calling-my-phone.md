---
title: Apartment buzzer not calling your phone? Causes and fixes
description: A visitor buzzes your unit and your phone never rings. Here are the usual reasons, how to check each one in a few minutes, and what to ask your building to fix.
updated: 2026-11-20
---
If your building's intercom is supposed to call your phone and it doesn't, the problem is almost always on the building's side or in your phone's settings, not a fault in your phone. Work through these in order.

## 1. Your phone is silencing the call

Calls from unknown numbers often don't ring when a Focus, Do Not Disturb, or "silence unknown callers" setting is on. Intercom calls come from a number you don't have saved.

- **iPhone:** check Settings, Focus, and Settings, Phone, Silence Unknown Callers. Either turn it off, or save the intercom's number as a contact and allow that contact through your Focus.
- **Android:** check Do Not Disturb, and in the Phone app's settings look for "Block unknown numbers" or "Caller ID and spam".

Have a friend buzz you, then check your recent calls and voicemail. If the call shows up there, it was silenced rather than missed.

## 2. The building has an old or wrong number for you

The panel dials a number stored in the building's system. If you changed phones, moved units, or the number was typed wrong, it is calling someone else, or nobody. Ask your property manager exactly which number is on file for your unit, and ask them to [update it](/docs/forward-apartment-buzzer-to-cell-phone).

## 3. The panel is calling a landline or a closed number

Some systems only call a landline, or a number that has been disconnected. If your unit still lists a landline you no longer have, no phone will ring.

## 4. Your carrier is filtering the call

Some carriers label or block automated calls. Check that your carrier's spam filter isn't set to block unlabeled callers, and try again from a different carrier's phone if you have one handy.

## 5. The intercom or the building is the problem

If the panel doesn't seem to be dialing at all (visitors say nothing happens, no ringing sound), the panel may be offline or out of service. Only the building can fix that.

## If you use Knock

If you installed Knock and the building's calls don't reach it, the usual cause is a Telnyx **Trial** account, which rejects calls from any number except the one you verified. See [Upgrade your Telnyx account to Paid](/docs/telnyx/upgrade), then the [troubleshooting page](/docs/troubleshooting).

## Test it yourself

Call your phone number from another phone first. If it rings, your number works and the problem is what the building has on file. If it doesn't, start at step 1.
