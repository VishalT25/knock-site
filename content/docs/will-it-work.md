---
title: Will it work with my intercom?
description: Knock needs your building's intercom to do two specific things. Here is how to find out in five minutes, before you set up anything or pay for anything.
updated: 2026-11-20
---
Knock does not replace your building's intercom. It sits in the middle of a phone call the intercom already makes. So whether Knock works depends entirely on **how your intercom behaves**, not on anything you install.

## What your building has to do

Your intercom must do both of these:

1. **Call a regular phone number when a visitor buzzes your unit.** Many apartment buildings work this way: you have a phone number "on file" for your unit, and the panel by the front door dials it.
2. **Unlock the door when it hears a key press on that call.** You answer, press a number (usually **9**, sometimes `#` or `*`), and the door buzzes open.

If both are true, Knock can answer that call instead of your personal phone, and press the digit for you when anyone in the household taps **Open door**.

There is also a practical third thing: someone has to be able to **change the phone number on file** for your unit (your property manager or the intercom company), or you need to be able to forward calls from the number that is on file. [Connect your building](/docs/connect-building) covers that.

## What does not work

- **Intercoms that ring a handset on your wall.** There is no phone call for Knock to answer.
- **Systems that only open from the vendor's own app** (no phone call, no key press). Knock can't press buttons inside another company's app.
- **Doors opened with a card, fob or keypad code only.**

If your intercom is one of these, Knock can't help, and it is better to find out now.

## Find out in five minutes

You don't need to set anything up for this. You only need to ask the right person the right questions, or watch what happens when a friend buzzes you.

### Option 1: Ask whoever receives the calls

Today one person in your household probably gets the intercom call on their phone. Ask them:

- When a visitor buzzes, does your **phone ring with a normal call**? (Not a notification from an app.)
- When you answer, what do you **press to open the door**? Write down the digit.
- Is there a **spoken prompt** first ("Press 9 to open the door")? That's fine; it just tells you the digit.

### Option 2: Do a live test

1. Ask a friend to stand at the front door and buzz your unit.
2. On the phone that receives calls, answer.
3. Press the digit. Does the door unlock?

If yes, you have confirmed both requirements.

### Option 3: Ask the building

Send your property manager or intercom company one question: *"When a visitor buzzes my unit, does the intercom call a phone number, and does pressing a digit on that call unlock the door?"* A template email is on the [connect your building](/docs/connect-building) page.

:::tip Write the digit down
Whatever you press to unlock is the **unlock digit** in Knock's settings. The default is `9`. If yours is different, you will change it in [Settings](/docs/settings) later.
:::

## Two details that trip people up

- **The intercom's own phone number doesn't matter.** Caller ID is irrelevant. Only the number *it dials* matters.
- **Ask if internet phone numbers are accepted.** A Telnyx number is an internet (VoIP) number. Almost every modern system dials it like any other number, but a few older systems only dial certain carriers. If your building says "cell phones and landlines only", check that VoIP numbers work before you pay for anything.

## Decision

| What you found | What it means |
|---|---|
| It calls a phone, a digit unlocks it, the number can be changed | Go ahead. [Costs and accounts](/docs/costs) is next. |
| It calls a phone and a digit unlocks it, but the building won't change the number | Likely works: forward calls from the number on file to your Knock number. See [Connect your building](/docs/connect-building). |
| It rings a wall handset, or only opens from an app | Knock can't help. |
| You don't know | Do Option 2 or 3 above. |
