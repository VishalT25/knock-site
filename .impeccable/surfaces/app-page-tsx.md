Mode: home = Persuade; docs = Read. Separate Next.js project, static. Established world: the Knock app's Liquid Glass language (DESIGN.md), inherited unchanged: warm gradient backdrop with orange glow, glass surfaces, orange glass key, dot-matrix (Doto) readouts, light/dark switch.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is a wall-mounted intercom panel, not a SaaS hero. The visitor "buzzes" the section they want. It refuses the split hero (copy left, product right, badge row) and the row of equal feature cards.
OWN-WORLD: The app's glass over warm peach/charcoal gradient, orange glass keys, Doto dot-matrix for readouts, system/Hanken type. Panel = one big glass plate with an LCD readout on top and a grid of glass buzzer buttons (one per section) below; the orange key is the Deploy call button. Content sections are glass plates on the same wall. Docs are calm glass reading pages.
STORY: Visitor reads the H1 on the LCD ("Open your apartment door from your phone"), sees the cost/effort honesty immediately, presses INTERCOM CHECK to see if their building works, tries the live demo phone, then deploys. Docs carry them through the long Telnyx setup with screenshots, using real mistakes people make.
